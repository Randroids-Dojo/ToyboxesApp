# Storefront design

The site's job is to show the two Android games, explain their gameplay immediately, and take visitors to the correct Google Play listing.

Palette: sky paper #edf6ff, navy ink #192d50, readable muted blue #4b6078, link blue #235bc4, toybox yellow #ffcf55, white #ffffff. The established Toyboxes icon contributes coral and green.

Type: the existing Toyboxes Lilita One display face for friendly, substantial headings; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for descriptions, controls and navigation. Both are served locally.

Layout: a left-aligned, two-line headline introduces two large gameplay-led game entries. A small yellow toybox shape communicates the payment model. Real screenshots carry the game-specific character; surrounding navigation stays quiet. Phone layouts stack entries and expose both navigation links without a hamburger menu.

Review against the brief: use the established Toyboxes icon and type instead of an unrelated studio identity. Use real captures instead of invented box art or device mockups. Keep purchase links close to genre descriptions. Put the town behind a normal link and keep its project separate. No autoplay video, loading gate or client-rendered catalog.

The Perspective Opus comparison starts with both screenshots in HTML; a small script adds two accessible view buttons. Removing JavaScript leaves the full information visible.
